Reference

How each number is built.

CTR

All clicks ÷ impressions. Includes every click on the ad, not just the button.

CPC

Spend ÷ clicks. What one click on the ad cost.

CPM

Spend ÷ impressions × 1,000. What a thousand views cost — the main driver of how much volume a fixed budget buys.

Click to lead

Form fills ÷ clicks. Measures the landing experience, since the ad has already done its job by the click.

Cost per lead

Spend ÷ form fills. Uses Meta's attributed lead count, before any human or AI qualification.

Frequency

Impressions ÷ people reached. How many times, on average, each person saw the ad.
