The form is where the intent goes missing.

53 clicks produced three form fills. 5.7% is low — a short instant form normally converts 15–30% of its clicks. People arrived, and then most of them stopped at the first question. That is the single biggest number to move this week.

Saturday bought almost nothing.

17 Oct produced 42 impressions and one click at R14.12 a click, while 18 Oct produced 16 clicks at R3.90. The gap isn't the ad itself — it's that at this budget a single day is 42 to 229 impressions, and one quiet day can't be told apart from a bad one.

Reference

How each number is built.

CTR

All clicks ÷ impressions. Includes every click on the ad, not just the button.

CPC

Spend ÷ clicks. What one click on the ad cost.

CPM

Spend ÷ impressions × 1,000. What a thousand views cost — the main driver of how much volume a fixed budget can buy.

Click to lead

Form fills ÷ clicks. Measures the landing experience, since the ad has already done its job by the click.

Cost per lead

Spend ÷ form fills. Uses Meta's attributed lead count, before any human or AI qualification.

Through to call

Leads the AI qualifier actually dialled. A call that fails on a carrier message still counts as dialled, not as reached.
